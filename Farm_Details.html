Irrigation Flow

  1. Phase 1: Germination and Establishment (0–60 Days)

    2. Goal: Ensure even bud sprouting and deep root hold.

    Action: Provide light, frequent irrigations.

    Frequency: Every 7 to 10 days depending on topsoil dryness and rainfall. Avoid heavy pooling that causes seedpiece rot.

  2. Phase 2: Tillering and Grand Growth (60–210 Days)

    3. Goal: Support rapid stalk elongation, canopy closure, and peak water demand.

    Action: Maintain consistent soil moisture in the top 60–90 cm root zone. Implement fertigation to split nitrogen and potassium applications directly through the irrigation setup.

    Frequency: Every 3 to 5 days for drip systems, or 10 to 12 days for furrow methods

  3. Phase 3: Ripening and Maturation (210+ Days to Harvest)

    4. Goal: Stress the plant slightly to restrict vegetative growth and boost sucrose accumulation.

    Action: Gradually widen irrigation intervals or withdraw watering entirely.

    Frequency: Reduce frequency significantly; stop all irrigation 3 to 4 weeks prior to harvest to ease field machinery access and raise commercial cane sugar (CCS) content.

Crop Stages

Stage 1: Germination and Establishment
Timing: Days 0 to 40 after planting.
Process: Roots and shoots sprout from the nodes (buds) of planted stem cuttings ("setts").
Key Needs: Warm soil temperatures (20–32°C) and consistent moisture.
Milestone: Ends when the young shoot establishes its primary roots and initial green leaves emerge.

Stage 2: Tillering
Timing: Days 40 to 120 after planting.
Process: Secondary shoots (tillers) emerge from the base of the primary plant.
Key Needs: Nitrogen and potassium fertilizers alongside regular watering to support shoot multiplication.
Milestone: Tiller stabilization occurs; only 40–50% of total tillers survive to become primary millable stalks.

Stage 3: Grand Growth (Elongation) Stage
Timing: Days 120 to 270 (for a 12-month crop).
Process: Rapid internode elongation and vertical stalk growth build the bulk of the crop's biomass and yield.
Key Needs: High sunlight, ample watering, and heavy nutrient inputs.
Milestone: Stalks add 4 to 5 new internodes per month under ideal conditions.

Stage 4: Maturation and Ripening Stage
Timing: Day 270 until harvest (10 to 18 months total, depending on region and variety).
Process: Vegetative growth slows down or stops; sucrose concentration shifts from reducing sugars to stable, high-purity sucrose in the stalks.
Key Needs: Reduced irrigation and cooler, drier weather to encourage sugar storage.
Milestone: The crop achieves optimal Brix levels (16–18%) and is ready for harvesting.
